2. What sur.plus does, and does not do

sur.plus is an intermediation platform. We match surplus goods you no longer need with verified recipients, coordinate the handover, and generate the documentation for it.

We never take ownership of your goods. The transfer is direct between you and the recipient. sur.plus is not a party to it, does not buy, sell, store or transport the goods, and does not act as your agent. Title, risk and any warranty pass directly between you and the recipient.

Handover, passing of risk and transport. The risk of accidental loss or deterioration passes when the goods are handed over to the recipient or to a person acting on its behalf; where you organise transport, on delivery to the recipient. For a gift to a tax-privileged recipient you are liable to that recipient only for intent and gross negligence under § 521 BGB, and for defects only where you concealed them fraudulently (§ 524 BGB); an accurate description of condition under section 5 is therefore your most important protection. Where we coordinate a pickup at your request (section 3), we act as coordinator: we are neither carrier nor freight forwarder. We commission a transport company in your name and for your account; the contract of carriage is between you and that company, which is liable under §§ 425 et seq. HGB. Packing and loading safe for carriage remain your responsibility (§ 412 HGB).

Donation receipt. Only the receiving tax-privileged organisation can issue it, on its own responsibility. We prepare it: from the details you and the recipient recorded for the match, meaning type and quantity of goods, the value applied, condition, handover date and recipient data, we generate a pre-filled draft that the recipient reviews, corrects where needed and signs digitally. Responsibility for accuracy and valuation stays with you and the recipient. The same applies to the impact and CO2 figures we calculate from your input.

We do not give tax or legal advice. The assessment of deductibility, VAT treatment (§ 3(1b) UStG) and reportability remains yours and your advisors’.

2a. Build-up phase: how sur.plus works today

sur.plus is being built. The first handovers have been running since autumn 2026; the application is being developed alongside. So that you know what you are getting, this is how the process works today:

Registration and access. You register through an online form or by e-mail to service@surpluspurpose.com. We set up an account in the application once the relevant area has been enabled for corporate customers; until then, your contact at sur.plus is your access.

Matching and handover. Matching is coordinated by people: we check your details, approach verified recipients, agree quantities, dates and transport with both sides and accompany the handover. Offers, commitments and handover dates are recorded in text form, usually by e-mail.

Documentation. The device or goods list, the handover record, the pre-filled draft of the donation receipt and the impact report are produced from your details and the documented handover. We deliver them as files (PDF, spreadsheet) until the application generates them for you; content and evidential value are the same.

Feature status at the time of contracting. The application features named in section 3, such as dashboard, copilot, newsletter or digital signature, are enabled step by step. We tell you in text form beforehand which of them are available at the time you contract (feature overview). Features not yet available we provide personally or as a file where their purpose allows; they are not a defect of the platform. What can be provided neither in the application nor personally is not owed for the duration of its unavailability and is reflected in the price we quote you.

Pilot character. Companies that work with us during the build-up phase get more personal support and less software than later customers will. Feedback on the process flows into development; no obligation to take part in product development or communication arises from this, and naming you remains subject to your consent under section 10.

3. Access and plans

Registration is free and opens the platform: you can browse what verified recipients currently need, including which organisation posted each need. Contact details and a coordinated match require a paid plan.

Pulse is free and covers the assessment only: impact potential score, potential report as a PDF, and an ESRS relevance overview. No account needed, no network access.

Seed covers up to 12 matches per calendar year, access to the verified recipient network, the donation overview in the dashboard, the automatically generated donation receipt digitally signed by the recipient, the AI copilot for listings and impact copy, 2 user seats, an onboarding call, and support responding within 48 hours.

Further matches in Seed. Beyond the twelve included matches we charge €290 per further match. We carry out such a match only after you have confirmed the additional charge in text form. Without that confirmation no claim arises and the match does not go ahead.

Tree covers everything in Seed, plus unlimited matches with curated priority matching, CSRD documentation per match with goods value, recipient and CO2e in audit-grade form (ISAE 3000, audit trail), access to the network of social service partners, extended copilot features, the weekly impact and needs newsletter, the marketing dashboard, 5 user seats and priority support.

Audit-grade CSRD documentation is part of Tree. Seed gives you the donation overview and the per-match donation receipt, not the full reporting set.

Logistics. Tree includes access to our vetted logistics partners: their rates, our packing specifications and the handover slot agreed with the recipient. You commission the transport yourself. Where we coordinate a pickup for you, we charge €390 per pickup. The transport cost itself is passed on without a mark-up, and we disclose the carrier’s invoice on request. In the project services described in the next paragraph, both are included in the fixed price.

Separately we offer two project services without a subscription. The First Donation Run covers one pickup at a fixed price of €2,900. One pickup means up to two pallets from one collection address on one agreed date, from one category family, such as IT or furniture or tools. How many recipients take the goods is neither limited nor charged for. Larger volumes, several sites or mixed categories are clearance and logistics, a one-off project at a fixed price set before the project starts. Not to be confused with the discounted transfer under section 6, which shares the word but concerns the receiving side.

Data erasure in a clearance project. Where a clearance project includes the erasure of data carriers, a certified partner named by us performs it. Because personal data may be processed on our instructions in the course of this, we conclude a data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR with you before the project starts, naming the partner as sub-processor. Erasure does not begin without that agreement. The erasure records per serial number become part of your project file.

Our undertaking for the First Donation Run. If we do not find a verified recipient for your goods within four weeks, the fixed price falls away entirely and we refund anything already paid. The period starts once the order is placed and your details on the type, quantity, condition and location of the goods are complete. The undertaking does not apply where you reject verified recipients we have named, where the goods do not match your description, or where the handover fails for reasons within your control. Expenses we incurred in advance at your express request, such as an on-site inspection, remain payable.

Credit. If you commission a clearance project within twelve months of a First Donation Run, we credit the fixed price paid for it in full against the price of that project.

Included subscription. The price of a clearance project includes one year of Seed, counted from the start of the project. That year does not renew automatically and does not roll over into a paid subscription by itself: it ends after twelve months unless you expressly continue it. We will remind you in text form at least 60 days before it expires.

Current prices are shown at surpluspurpose.com/#pricing. For companies above roughly 500 employees or with multiple sites we agree Scale and Enterprise terms separately.

Matching is human-coordinated during the build-up phase (section 2a). We do not owe a match: it depends on whether a verified recipient needs what you have.

5. What we ask of you

Only list goods you own and are entitled to dispose of, that are usable for their purpose, and that are lawful to transfer. Leased or rented items and items subject to retention of title are not among them. Do not list recalled, unsafe, expired or sanctioned items. Describe condition accurately and name known defects: a recipient plans around it, the donation receipt is based on it, and a named defect is not a concealed one.

Identity of the goods. State manufacturer, model and, where available, serial or batch number per line item. This is the basis of the donation receipt, enables recall checks and allows you to name the manufacturer in the event of a product-related damage claim (§ 4(3) ProdHaftG).

Data and licences. Do not list devices that still hold personal data or that are enrolled in device management or protected by an activation lock. Wiping IT hardware is your responsibility unless it is expressly part of a clearance project under section 3. Pass on installed software only where the licence may transfer with the device, and record the licence position.

New goods and returns. Where you release unsold goods or returns, you remain the economic operator for those goods under Regulation (EU) 2023/988. You ensure that the goods meet the safety and labelling requirements applicable to them, supply manufacturer details, warnings and instructions, and tell us without delay about any recall or safety warning concerning listed goods; we then inform the recipients concerned.

Materials and semi-finished goods. Where you list materials such as film, timber, metal or fabric, you confirm that they are goods for further use and not waste within the meaning of § 3 KrWG, and that they contain no hazardous substances. Supply a data sheet or the essential material details. For materials that could come into contact with food, state whether a declaration of conformity under Art. 16 of Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 exists; otherwise we mark the goods as not intended for food contact.

6. Discounted transfer to small businesses and private individuals

Recipients are usually tax-privileged organisations and public or church-run bodies. Small businesses and private individuals running a public-benefit project may also receive surplus. The usual route is a discounted transfer for a symbolic price against an invoice, because an outright gift to these groups is not a deductible donation and may count as taxable income for the recipient. An outright gift remains possible if you expressly want that; it then carries no donation effect. You choose the route in the offer.

For a paid transfer, the sale is concluded directly between you and the recipient. sur.plus intermediates, is not the seller and does not become a party to the sale. You are the seller, with the obligations that follow.

The contract is not concluded through the application. The application makes the introduction, displays your offer and documents the handover. Offer and acceptance are exchanged directly between you and the recipient, outside the application, for instance by e-mail or at an inspection; we provide full contact details for that purpose. sur.plus therefore does not operate an online marketplace within the meaning of § 312l(2) BGB or Art. 3(14) of Regulation (EU) 2023/988. Should the flow change, we will inform you in advance.

Towards consumers (§ 13 BGB) this means in particular: statutory liability for defects applies. For used goods it may be shortened to one year but, under § 476(2) BGB, not excluded. Where you conclude the contract at a distance, for instance by e-mail, the information duties under Art. 246a EGBGB and the duty to instruct on the right of withdrawal also apply to you. sur.plus provides a model instruction and model withdrawal form for this (see withdrawal) and flags it in the flow once the recipient is a consumer. Correct provision remains your responsibility.

Product safety. Regulation (EU) 2023/988 (GPSR) covers the supply, for payment or free of charge, of products intended for or likely to be used by consumers, including used products. You bear the due-diligence duties of a distributor (Art. 12 GPSR). Do not offer goods subject to a recall or safety warning, leave labelling and instructions on the product, clearly mark goods that need repair before use as such, and tell us about known safety defects without delay. We maintain a product safety contact point at service@surpluspurpose.com and forward reports to you.

Electrical equipment. Transferring used electrical and electronic equipment to private individuals may trigger duties under ElektroG and battery law. Check this before listing; sur.plus does not assume registration or take-back duties on your behalf.
