How we work

Evidenced, not asserted

Every guide states the legal basis, the source and the date it reflects. Primary sources, the Official Journal, the Commission, national statistics offices — take precedence, and secondary sources are labelled as such.

Gaps stay gaps

Where we could not verify something, we say so. For instance: no EU-level guidance extends the “taxable amount could be zero” position beyond food, and no EU register of charitable recipients exists.

AI-assisted, editorially reviewed

Drafts are produced with AI assistance and checked against the primary sources before publication. Who is responsible, what is checked and what explicitly is not: Editorial standards.

More in German

Where the answer is national rather than EU-wide: German tax law, donation receipts, office clearance and the German original of the nonprofit guide, the Ratgeber section covers it in ten German-language guides.

These guides are editorial orientation and do not constitute legal, tax or data protection advice. EU regulations apply directly; directives are transposed nationally and national practice diverges. For binding advice, consult advisers in the member state concerned. Concrete matches are shown in our case studies.
