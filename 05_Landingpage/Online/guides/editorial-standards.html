Editorial standards: how this content is produced

The guides on this site are produced with AI assistance and editorially reviewed before publication. This page sets out what that means in practice: who is responsible, where the statements come from, what is checked — and what explicitly is not.

Last updated: 12 August 2026 Responsible: Florian Lechner Applies to: all guide and Ratgeber pages

Who is responsible

Editorial responsibility for all guide content on surpluspurpose.com rests with Florian Lechner, sur.plus, Landsberger Strasse 57, 82266 Inning am Ammersee-Stegen, Germany, reachable at service@surpluspurpose.com. Responsibility for publication therefore sits with a named natural person — not with a tool, and not with an anonymous editorial desk.

How an article is produced

Every piece goes through the same process:

  1. Topic selection. Topics come from concrete questions raised by companies and nonprofits, from Google Search Console data, and from regulatory deadlines that are becoming relevant.
  2. Source research. Research is done against primary sources: statutory texts, the actual wording of EU regulations via EUR-Lex, German Federal Ministry of Finance circulars, official templates and publications of the competent authorities. Secondary literature is used for context, not as evidence.
  3. Drafting. The draft is produced with AI assistance. Structure, argument and wording are machine-proposed at this stage.
  4. Editorial review. Before publication every load-bearing statement is read back against the cited source. Claims without a citable source are removed or marked as an assessment.
  5. Publication with sources. Every article lists its sources and states the legal position it relies on.
What is not reviewed

This content has not been reviewed by lawyers, tax advisers or auditors. It does not replace legal or tax advice in an individual case. Valuation, bookkeeping, VAT and evidence requirements must be settled with your own advisers — particularly outside Germany, where the rules differ. A professional review is being prepared; once it is in place it will be stated here and in the relevant article.

How AI is used — and how it is not

AI is used for research support, structuring and drafting. It does not decide what gets published. Nothing goes online unread.

Explicitly not machine-generated: figures taken from primary sources, quotations, statutory references and deadlines — each is checked individually against the source. Where imagery is AI-generated, it is labelled at the image.

This page also documents the human review and editorial responsibility within the meaning of Article 50(4) of Regulation (EU) 2024/1689 (the AI Act), whose transparency obligations have applied since 2 August 2026.

Currency and corrections

Every article states the legal position date it relies on. If the law changes, the article is revised and the date updated; substantive changes are marked in the text rather than silently replaced.

If you spot an error: service@surpluspurpose.com. Substantiated corrections are incorporated promptly.

Independence

There are no paid placements, no advertorials and no affiliate links. Links to external sources are editorial and unpaid. sur.plus operates a platform for in-kind donations and therefore has an evident interest in the subject — where an article points to our own offering, it is marked as such and kept separate from the editorial part.
