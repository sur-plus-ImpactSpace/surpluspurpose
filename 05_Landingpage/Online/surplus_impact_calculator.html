Methodology
Disposal cost: your selected rate × estimated weight (derived from €/kg per category - same weight logic as the impact section). Logistics reduction: 45% saving on inbound logistics vs. disposal collection. Tax effect compared with disposal: disposal also writes off the residual book value, and a donation at book value cancels out the withdrawal value. What counts on top is therefore only the VAT on the donation (a cost; assessed on second-hand market value, using the same benchmark as the selling route: IT 15%, tools 10%, furniture 5%, textiles 2%, stationery 0% of the value entered) and its deduction within the donation deduction; in France and Spain the credit on the donation value. Consult your tax advisor. One-off surplus gets a fixed-price quote before the project starts instead of a subscription. sur.plus plans: Seed €990/yr · Tree €3,890/yr · Enterprise (custom, not publicly priced). Storage (new): weight → pallets (300 kg) → floor space (1.5 m² per pallet incl. aisle) × months × €/m²/month (default €12, i.e. €80-200/m²/year). Internal time: 3 h per pallet, minimum 4 h, × hourly rate; about 30% of that with sur.plus. Selling: indicative proceeds per category on replacement value (IT 15%, tools 10%, furniture 5%, textiles 2%, stationery 0%) - for young IT with residual value, selling is often the better deal; donating creates a documented recipient instead. Returns & new goods (new): the surplus value is taken as purchase value. VAT on the donation = purchase value × condition factor (worthless 0, limited marketability 0.5 as an assumption, fully saleable 1) × standard VAT rate of the country; for France, Spain and the United Kingdom the local VAT relief for donated goods is assumed (€0). Tax effect compared with destruction: destroyed goods also reduce profit; in deduction countries only the deduction of the VAT on the donation comes on top, in France and Spain the credit on the reduced value. Selling: stock-buyer proceeds as a benchmark of 15% (saleable) or 5% (limited) of purchase value. Document the actual reduction case by case. All figures are estimates for indicative purposes only. Build-up phase: prices show the long-term structure.
