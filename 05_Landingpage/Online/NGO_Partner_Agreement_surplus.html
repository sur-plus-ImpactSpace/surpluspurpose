sur.plus Platform — Pre-Registration
What is sur.plus? A B2B platform that matches corporate surplus goods (returns, B-stock, overstock) with verified NGOs, at no cost to NGOs. Every match generates automated CSRD/ESG documentation for the corporate donor. NGOs receive resources they need, without fundraising or cold outreach to companies. The platform is currently being built. This agreement registers your organisation in the verified NGO network ahead of the first matches.

This is a straightforward pre-registration document. It records your organisation’s details, resource needs, and the mutual commitments between sur.plus UG (haftungsbeschränkt), Landsberger Straße 57, 82266 Inning am Ammersee-Stegen, Germany, registered with the commercial register of the Munich Local Court under HRB 315904 (“sur.plus”), and your NGO. C — Mutual Commitments
What sur.plus commits to
  1. NGO access is free of charge: no fees, no subscriptions, no commission on goods received.
  2. Priority match notifications: you are notified as soon as surplus matching your needs becomes available — ahead of the wider network.
  3. Logistics coordination with the corporate partner. Transport costs are as a rule borne by the donating company; deviations, such as collection by you, are agreed per match before you confirm. Risk passes to you on handover; under German law the donor is liable to you only for intent and gross negligence (sec. 521 BGB). Condition and known defects are recorded in the handover protocol.
  4. A verified impact report for every match — usable for grant applications and donor reporting.
  5. GDPR-compliant data processing. EU-based storage. Your data is never sold or shared without consent.

Version 2.1, effective 10 October 2026 (transport costs “as a rule”, passing of risk, categories aligned with the platform’s exclusion list: no food, no medical devices). For partners who registered before 13 August 2026, version 1.0 and its commitment to permanently free access continue to apply unchanged; for matches already confirmed, the transport arrangement promised at the time applies.

What we ask of you
  1. Confirm receipt of allocated goods within 5 business days of delivery via the sur.plus confirmation process.
  2. Use goods received solely for the charitable purposes stated in your registration. No commercial resale.
  3. Respond to match notifications within 48 hours where possible. Repeated non-response may affect priority status.
  4. Notify sur.plus of material changes (address, legal status, capacity) within 14 days.
  5. Consent to sur.plus referencing your organisation in anonymised impact communications. Whether we may name you beyond that is your own choice in Section D.
Term & termination

This agreement is effective from the date of signature and continues indefinitely. Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice to service@surpluspurpose.com. Governing law: Germany. Disputes resolved by good-faith negotiation first.

D — Data & Consent

sur.plus processes organisation name, contact details, resource needs and match history to operate the platform. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR. EU-based storage. Retained for partnership duration plus 3 years. Deleted on written request after termination.
