About this report

This document constitutes the CSRD-aligned impact documentation for surplus goods donated via the sur.plus platform during the reporting period. Every data point is derived from verified, completed transactions. NGO confirmations are digitally signed. CO2 figures follow the GHG Protocol Corporate Value Chain (Scope 3) Standard. Diverting surplus from disposal reduces Category 5, waste generated in operations; where the donating entity pays for transport, those emissions fall under Category 4. This report is formatted for use in your ESRS E5, S3 and G1 disclosures.

Verification standard: All impact figures are based on completed, NGO-confirmed transactions. CO2 avoided figures use life-cycle emission factors per product category (GHG Protocol). Monetary values reflect the fair value of the goods at the date of transfer, as declared by the donating entity - the basis a German donation receipt requires. The original procurement value is held separately in your transaction export. This report provides documentation intended for use within your ESRS disclosures under CSRD (EU 2022/2464).
